The Problem

Textbooks are where knowledge goes to be forgotten.

Kids don't hate learning. They hate sitting still while facts are read at them from a page they'll close and never reopen.

So flip it. What if the exact same science, history, and math a student is tested on was the only thing standing between a team of friends and disaster?

The Idea

Turn the entire curriculum into an anime — where every lesson is a weapon.

Same facts. Same syllabus. Zero boredom.

The concepts a student is graded on become the knowledge five friends need to survive each adventure — earned in the story, never lectured at the viewer.

The engine is simple: knowledge drives the plot. The heroes literally can't move forward until they figure something out.

And because it's built from the national curriculum, kids are bingeing the chapter they'd never read.

Season One · رحلة عبر التاريخ

Five eras of Bahrain. Every subject in the curriculum.

Each arc is a real chapter of Bahraini history — and each is anchored to real lessons in the national curriculum.

Dilmun ~2000 BC

The land of life is running dry

The sweet-water springs that make Dilmun livable are turning to salt. Understand where the fresh water comes from — and why it's failing — or the first civilization falls.

ScienceEarth science

Anchored in: Science Gr.6 · Social Studies Gr.6 (Dilmun)

Pearls ~1800s–1930s

Lost at sea, out of air

A diving dhow can't find its way home or coordinate the dive. Navigate by the stars, master pressure and breath — and the sea-songs that keep the crew alive.

PhysicsAstronomyMusic

Anchored in: Science Gr.6 (pearls) · Music (the diving songs)

Faith 7th c. CE

A message that must be understood

The coming of Islam to Bahrain — Al-Ala al-Hadrami. A dispute settled not by force but by eloquence, wisdom, and trust (الأمانة).

ArabicIslamicHistoryCivics

Anchored in: Social Studies & Arabic Gr.7 (Al-Ala al-Hadrami)

Golden Age 9th–11th c.

The house of wisdom, going blank

A great calculation and a great cure are vanishing as their inventors are erased. Rebuild them with the algebra of al-Khwarizmi and the optics of Ibn al-Haytham.

Math · AlgebraPhysics · Optics

Anchored in: Math Gr.9 (al-Khwarizmi) · Science Gr.9

Now Oil → AI

Why It Works

Curriculum-aligned = trust

Parents and teachers back it because it maps to exactly what's studied and tested. Same syllabus, zero boredom.

An unfair advantage

The entire national curriculum — digitized, cleaned, structured. A knowledge bank no competitor can copy.

Safe by design

Original stories that teach the concepts — not reproductions of the books. You own the world you build.

Local heart, global shape

Bahraini history and dialect earn deep local love; the adventure format travels far beyond the Gulf.

The Plan

De-risked, in three moves

Step 01

Write the story

The scripts and world — near-free to produce, and the part nobody can copy. This is the moat.

Step 02

Tease & prove it cheaply

Short teasers and a motion-comic scene on YouTube & Instagram. Measure if the audience shows up.

Step 03

Animate what wins

Only produce full animation for what the audience has already proven it wants.

Nothing expensive happens until the idea earns it.
